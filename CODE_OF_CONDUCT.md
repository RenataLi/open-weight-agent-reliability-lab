# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and technically rigorous community. Contributors of
all backgrounds and experience levels should be able to discuss ideas, challenge assumptions, and
review evidence without harassment or personal attacks.

## Expected behavior

- be constructive, precise, and generous in technical discussion;
- critique implementations and evidence, not people;
- respect privacy, attribution, and responsible disclosure;
- acknowledge uncertainty and correct mistakes transparently;
- keep benchmark discussion reproducible and evidence-based.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, deliberate disclosure of private information,
plagiarism, or sustained disruption are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

For a sensitive conduct concern, use the repository's private vulnerability-reporting form and
prefix the report title with `Conduct`; do not open a public issue. Maintainers may edit or remove
contributions and restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

This policy is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/),
version 2.1.
